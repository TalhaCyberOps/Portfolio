01 / About

Cybersecurity first.
AI is where I apply it.

Cybersecurity is my discipline. I earned a BS in Cyber Security from Air University, Islamabad (2022–2026), and I am currently pursuing an MS in Cyber Security there while working as an AI Engineer Intern.

My work sits where AI and security meet: hardening LLM applications against prompt injection, building detection pipelines where AI interprets findings but does not make security decisions, and securing edge networks with cryptography and on-device intrusion detection. I also bring a governance perspective through ISO/IEC 27001, risk assessment and compliance documentation.

  • Evidence over assertion. Every verdict traces back to a record.
  • Deterministic controls around probabilistic models. Code validates what the model says.
  • Defence in depth. An attack should have to beat every layer, not one.
  • Honest limits. Known gaps are documented, not hidden.

03 / Selected work

Built, tested, documented.

Flagship · Open source · v1.0.1

AEGISINTEL

Evidence-first SOC investigation and threat-intelligence platform.

A local-first platform where deterministic rules and statistics produce findings and a local LLM only interprets them. Three independent detection layers, an evidence-linked incident model, validated ATT&CK mapping, and human-approved, simulated response.

  • Sigma-subset rules, correlation rules and explainable behavioural analytics that record coverage gaps rather than inventing baselines
  • AI reports validated in code: schema, evidence IDs and ATT&CK IDs; prompt-injection scanner; deterministic fallback
  • Argon2id authentication, server-side sessions, RBAC, two-person approval, SHA-256 hash-chained audit log
  • Hostile-input-safe ingestion with provenance; verified backups and audited restore

AEGIS-LN

AI-protected LoRa mesh communication system.

A team-developed, decentralised, infrastructure-independent LoRa mesh with AOMDV-inspired multipath routing, ECDH session key exchange, AES-128-CBC encryption and HMAC-SHA256 authentication, plus autoencoder and deep-packet-inspection anomaly detection with a real-time threat dashboard.

Secure Policy RAG Assistant

Prompt-injection-hardened, fully local RAG assistant.
